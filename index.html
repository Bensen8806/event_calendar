Process

How venue booking works

Every event request follows a defined two-level approval path to ensure fairness, conflict prevention, and institutional accountability.

01
Select Venue on Map

Choose a date range, then click any venue on the interactive campus map. Green means available, amber means pending, red means booked.

02
Submit Event Request

Club Heads fill in event details — title, description, club, category, expected attendance, and KTU activity point segment if applicable.

03
HOD Reviews Request

The HOD of the department that owns the venue reviews the request. A written remark is mandatory for all decisions. Approved requests proceed to the Principal.

04
Principal Final Approval

The Principal provides the final decision with a mandatory remark. Once approved, the event is published and the venue is marked booked for that slot.

Access Control

Role-based access

Each user has a defined role that determines what they can see and do. A user can hold multiple roles simultaneously.

Admin
Manages users, roles, departments, and venue records across the entire system.
Principal
Views all events. Provides final approval or rejection with a mandatory remark.
HOD
Reviews venue requests for their department only. Approves or rejects with a mandatory remark.
Club Head
Creates events, selects venues on the map, tracks approval status, and marks attendance.
Student
Browses approved events, registers, and downloads participation certificates.
